# A Hybrid Framework for Quantum Encryption Decryption

> Integrating Quantum Key Distribution with Post Quantum Cryptography

**Author:**
Niladri Biswas

*Master of Information Security, Department of Information Technology,
Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology, West Bengal, India*

**Keywords:**
Quantum encryption, quantum decryption, quantum key distribution (QKD), post quantum cryptography (PQC), hybrid cryptosystems, lattice based cryptography, security proofs

**Abstract**
This paper proposes a novel hybrid framework that integrates quantum key distribution (QKD) with classical post quantum cryptographic (PQC) algorithms. With the growing threat of quantum computers to conventional encryption schemes (such as RSA and ECC), our work addresses the dual challenges of achieving information theoretic security while maintaining computational efficiency. The framework leverages QKD protocols (like BB84 and device independent QKD) to generate one time pads and uses lattice based schemes (e.g., CRYSTALS–Kyber) for key encapsulation. We include theoretical analyses, extensive simulation results (with key parameters such as a 50 km optical fiber channel, 3.8% average QBER, and ~10 kbit/s key rate), and rigorous security proofs. Our results indicate that even if one layer is compromised, the overall system remains robust—much like having two locks on a door to keep intruders out.
